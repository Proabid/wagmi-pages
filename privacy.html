Privacy Policy

Wagmi is built with privacy as a default. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and what stays on your device.

⚡ Short version: Wagmi does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your coin list lives only on your device.

Data we collect

Wagmi collects no personal data. The app does not require an account or login of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The only data stored locally on your device is your coin watchlist — the coins you add or remove. This data never leaves your device and is stored using iOS's standard UserDefaults mechanism.

Third-party services

Wagmi connects to the following external services to fetch market data:

  • Binance WebSocket — real-time price streaming. Your IP address is used to establish the connection, as with any internet request. Binance's privacy policy applies.
  • CoinGecko API — coin metadata and search. Requests are made directly from your device. CoinGecko's privacy policy applies.
  • TradingView — price charts rendered inside the app via a web view. TradingView's privacy policy applies.

We have no control over the data these services may collect independently. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

Analytics and tracking

Wagmi contains no analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising identifiers, or crash reporting tools. We do not know how you use the app.

Children

Wagmi is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
