Frequently asked questions

Prices are showing — instead of a number
This means the app hasn't received price data yet. It usually resolves within a few seconds after launch. If it persists, check your internet connection and relaunch the app. Some coins may take up to 10 seconds if they are not listed on Binance and need to be fetched from a backup source.
The chart isn't loading
Charts are powered by TradingView and require an internet connection. Make sure you're online and try tapping on the coin again to reopen the panel. If TradingView is experiencing an outage, charts may be temporarily unavailable.
How do I add a coin?
Tap the + button in the top right corner of the main screen. Search for any coin by name or ticker symbol and tap it to add it to your list.
How do I remove a coin?
Swipe left on any coin row in the list and tap Delete.
How do I reorder my coins?
Press and hold anywhere on the list to enter reorder mode. Drag the coins into the order you want using the handle on the right side of each row. Press and hold again to exit reorder mode.
What does the badge number mean when I tap it?
Tapping the percentage badge switches between showing the 24-hour percentage change and the 24-hour price change in USD. Tap again to switch back.
Will I lose my coins if I update the app?
No. Your coin list is saved locally on your device and persists through updates. It is only lost if you delete the app entirely.
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
Tap the gear icon in the top left corner of the main screen. You can toggle between light and dark mode in Settings.
Where does the price data come from?
Prices are streamed in real time from Binance via WebSocket, which means updates happen in under a second. For coins not listed on Binance, prices are fetched from CoinGecko and refreshed every 10 seconds.
